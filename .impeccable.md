## Design Context

### Users
Corporate employees commuting together. Mobile-first, used on the go.
Job: find a trusted coworker carpool quickly before the morning commute.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, efficient, community-first.
Three words: **reliable, connected, clean**.

### Aesthetic Direction
Blue backgrounds (hero, headers) + green CTAs/buttons.
White/off-white surfaces for content cards.
No decorative blobs, bubbles, or glassmorphism.
Clean, professional, high-contrast.

### Design Principles
1. Blue structures the app — backgrounds, hero, nav emphasis
2. Green drives action — every primary button, active tab, CTA
3. White surfaces breathe — cards and content stay on white
4. Zero decoration — no blobs, glow effects, or noise
5. Consistent throughout — same pattern on every screen
